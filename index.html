FAQs

Questions we get asked

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us and we'll help.

Which services do you offer?

Double glazing (new windows, doors and misted-glass repairs), solar panel installation and maintenance including batteries, electrical work from repairs to full rewires and EICRs, and HVAC: boilers, heat pumps and air conditioning.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover the major UK cities listed above and the surrounding towns. Enter your postcode in the coverage checker or the booking form and we'll confirm.

Are your engineers qualified?

Yes. Every engineer is ID-checked, insured and registered for their trade: Gas Safe for gas work, NICEIC or NAPIT for electrics, MCS for solar and heat pumps, FENSA for windows and F-Gas for air conditioning.

How does pricing work?

The inspection is free and there's no call-out charge for booked visits. After diagnosing the job, the engineer gives you a fixed written quote. You only pay once you've approved it.

Do you supply the parts and equipment?

Yes. We supply quality parts and branded equipment with manufacturer warranties, itemised on your quote. If you've already bought a part, let us know when booking.

What does the guarantee cover?

Our 12-month workmanship guarantee covers the work our engineer carried out. If the same fault comes back, we return and fix it free. Parts and new installations also carry manufacturer warranties.

Can you help with grants for solar or heat pumps?

Solar panels and batteries are currently VAT-free until 31 March 2027, and the Boiler Upgrade Scheme offers £7,500 towards a heat pump in England and Wales. We'll check what applies to you during your survey.
